# Celesphonia Modifier Project Operating Model

**Status:** Confirmed planning baseline

**Initial delivery posture:** Personal and private use only

**Scope:** Investment, sequencing, authority, design, assurance, delivery, and retirement

## 1. Role of this document

This document governs whether, when, and to what depth the project implements the candidate
requirements in `celesphonia-modifier-plan.md`. The technical plan remains the detailed source
for product, UX, transaction, recovery, and test hypotheses. The operating model overrides its
older `6+1` workstream and mega-Phase-0 progression where they conflict.

`project-perspective-map.md` remains the decision-routing taxonomy. Its 20 perspectives are not
20 teams, phases, backlogs, or approval meetings.

## 2. Confirmed product posture

The first deliverable is for one owner's personal and private use:

- Do not distribute binaries, installers, saves, diagnostics, or game-derived material.
- Do not purchase a signing identity or operate a public distribution channel yet.
- Do not make external support, update, language, or compatibility promises.
- Preserve production-grade save safety, losslessness, recovery, and operation evidence.
- Treat external testing or public distribution as a future investment decision, not an
  implicit next step.

The first proven Gold experiment established that structured editing is feasible. It did not
prove the semantics or write authority of every save domain. The next product question is
therefore not another UI feature. It is a comprehensive preliminary map of the observed save
structure, followed by human selection of valuable domains for deeper research.

## 3. Operating principles

1. Prove that a capability is valuable and semantically understood before building mature
   architecture around it.
2. Learn through small integrated outcomes rather than stream-local completion.
3. Keep read-only learning independent from synthetic writer research.
4. Qualify each writable operation separately for one exact compatibility scope.
5. Preserve complete source documents and unknown data.
6. Cut scope before lowering evidence, safety, rights, privacy, or assurance standards.
7. Bind release evidence to exact bytes if distribution is ever activated.
8. Treat interaction, content, accessibility, localization, and recovery comprehension as
   safety work.
9. Keep governance small: one decision log, one risk register, one evidence manifest, and
   runnable reviews.
10. Start support and retirement work before the first external tester, if external exposure is
    ever authorized.

## 4. Execution model

One cross-functional team works through five integrated execution tracks:

- **Product, stewardship, and economics:** User value, scope, responsible use, privacy, rights,
  cost, and continuation.
- **Domain, format, and compatibility truth:** Save representation, game semantics,
  fingerprints, parser, and corpus.
- **Mutation, recovery, and storage safety:** Hazard analysis, writer kernel, recovery, and
  Windows storage behavior.
- **Product experience and WinUI implementation:** Research synthesis, IA, flows, Fluent UI,
  accessibility, localization, and app implementation.
- **Release, deployment, and supported life:** Build, signing, package lifecycle, support,
  incident response, and retirement.

These tracks are delivery groupings, not independent departments. A small team may combine
roles, but every decision must state which accountability the person is exercising.

Independent Assurance is cross-cutting. It is not part of an evidence-producing track and may
not implement or materially author evidence it later approves.

## 5. Independent authority

- **Rights:** Decides fixture use, reverse-engineering boundaries, licensing, naming, and
  distribution permission. It cannot trade permission for schedule or approve a conflicted
  matter.
- **Privacy:** Decides diagnostic, support-data, retention, deletion, and stewardship
  boundaries. It cannot expand collection merely because support would be easier.
- **Safety:** Decides mutation, recovery, storage controls, and unsafe field conditions. It
  cannot lower semantic or rights evidence requirements.
- **Independent Assurance:** Decides evidence sufficiency, independence, freshness, and
  exact-artifact recommendations. It cannot approve evidence it materially authored.
- **Release:** Decides promotion or withdrawal of one exact approved candidate. It cannot
  rebuild, change, or waive a non-waivable gate.

An accountable maintainer controls investment, scope, and waivable residual risk. That role
cannot overrule a failed Rights, Privacy, Safety, or Independent Assurance gate.

Before external delivery, every authority and backup must be named. Rights records must define
qualification, mandatory-counsel triggers, recusal, escalation, and conflict handling.

## 6. Outcome stages

Stages describe temporary outcomes. They are not nine permanent processes.

| Stage | Outcome                                                        |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| S0    | Stewardship, authority, product posture, and capacity frame    |
| S1    | Product, semantic, deployment, and other kill-risk convergence |
| S2    | Signed read-only learning build                                |
| S3    | Disposable writer laboratory                                   |
| S4    | Integrated Gold alpha and E3 candidate                         |
| S5    | Invite-only writable beta                                      |
| S6    | Exact-artifact release candidate and release rehearsal         |
| S7    | Narrow launch                                                  |
| S8    | Supported life and continue, narrow, or sunset review          |

For the confirmed private posture:

- S0, S1, S3, and private parts of S4 remain relevant.
- S2 may be implemented as a local read-only walking slice without external exposure.
- S5 through S8 are dormant until a new investment decision authorizes distribution.
- Dormant stages must not cause signing, support, channel, or cohort work now.

## 7. Four commitment gates

Only four decisions authorize additional spend or exposure:

| Gate                | Decision                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| G0 Invest           | Select writable investigation, read-only, internal research, or stop     |
| G1 Expose Read-Only | Permit one exact signed read-only build to reach external participants   |
| G2 Expose Writes    | Permit one exact E3 Gold build to write participant copies               |
| G3 Release          | Promote one exact candidate with a dated support and retirement contract |

The current private posture does not require G1 or G2 for the owner's private use. Those gates
become applicable only after a new G0 explicitly changes the distribution posture.

No recurring committees or perspective-by-perspective approval sequence exists. Working reviews
occur weekly; gate reviews and stop reviews are event driven.

## 8. Critical path

The private writable critical path is:

```text
lawful evidence access
  -> comprehensive Atlas v0 structure survey
  -> human value selection
  -> focused semantic research
  -> E2 disposable-copy authorization
  -> lossless candidate boundary
  -> proportional writer and storage proof
  -> independent model and fault evidence
  -> integrated E3 operation
  -> recovery comprehension
```

E2 permits only controlled research writes to disposable copies with named operators and a
non-distributable build. E3 is required before external or original user-data writes.

The read-only path is shorter:

```text
lawful evidence access
  -> parser containment
  -> minimal inspect journey
  -> privacy-safe diagnostics
  -> local read-only product
```

## 9. Product design decision

Do not create a separate visual-design team or workstream. Standard WinUI and Fluent controls
appropriately constrain the visual surface, but they do not remove design work.

Reserve a named product-design and WinUI owner:

- approximately 0.2 to 0.4 FTE through read-only and writable learning;
- an initial three-to-five-working-day flow, IA, and content review;
- embedded reviews on each integrated increment; and
- five to ten working days of usability, accessibility, localization, and visual hardening
  before an external writable candidate.

Add a part-time product designer only if repeated usability work reveals material comprehension
failure, design churn blocks implementation, or no core member can own Fluent, UI Automation,
High Contrast, text scaling, and bilingual safety presentation together.

For any future bilingual external claim, run critical tasks separately in `en-US` and
`zh-Hans`. Do not borrow evidence between locale cohorts.

## 10. Writer proportionality

Do not adopt the existing rich replica and ledger design merely because it is detailed.
Before selecting a writer:

1. compare an archive-first two-file writer, a reduced mirrored-journal design, and the richer
   proposal;
2. state the unique hazard each mechanism controls;
3. freeze latency, maximum duration, write amplification, bytes-per-transaction, annual growth,
   recovery-state, profile, and fault-coverage limits before comparison; and
4. simplify or stop writable scope when no candidate passes prospectively declared limits.

Historical Restore, broad Reconcile, automatic retention deletion, cross-version readers, and
additional write operations are separate investments. They are not prerequisites for the first
safe Gold Save.

## 11. Capacity and timing

The preferred future delivery scenario is four to five core FTE plus fractional independent
specialists. This is not a staffing commitment for the current personal research phase.

Do not present a launch date until a resource-loaded schedule includes:

- named stage capacity and backups;
- specialist reservation dates;
- external dependency start-to-ready criteria;
- review and rework allowance;
- support and cross-training load;
- 15 to 20 percent schedule and cost contingency; and
- an automatic scope response for each missed assumption.

The previously discussed six-to-nine-month range is not a commitment.

External waiting may pause an elapsed stage once, only through the dependency's declared maximum
allowance plus two calendar weeks. It cannot expand person-week limits. A missed bound removes
the dependent scope, selects a lower product rung, or stops the project.

## 12. Evidence and lifecycle boundaries

Maintain one generated evidence manifest. It references product, corpus, parser, hazard,
model, fault, operation, design, accessibility, language, rights, privacy, security, artifact,
support, economics, and retirement evidence. Stage dossiers are views of this manifest, not
independent document systems.

For offline clients:

- locally detectable fingerprint or profile changes fail closed to read-only;
- channel withdrawal prevents new acquisition but cannot remotely disable installed bytes;
- signer, rights, or newly discovered semantic incidents require a user-acquired signed update;
  and
- any future promise must disclose this unsupported-write exposure window.

If distribution is activated, S6 must qualify and escrow a releasable read-only recovery
artifact or an exact reproducible recovery build path. Support, recovery availability, archive
retention, security response, and signing or account custody all require funded end dates.

## 13. Scope-cut ladder

Select the highest rung whose value, evidence, rights, capacity, and economics pass:

1. Gold writable product for one exact scope.
2. Read-only inspect and diagnosis product.
3. Internal research tool.
4. Stop, delete private working data, and archive only permitted records.

Never lower a safety or evidence criterion to preserve a higher rung.

## 14. Decisions complete now

The following decisions are confirmed:

- personal and private first delivery;
- five execution tracks and cross-cutting Independent Assurance;
- four commitment gates and one evidence manifest;
- no separate visual-design team;
- Gold remains the first candidate write;
- comprehensive Atlas v0 research precedes broader feature selection;
- E2 is disposable-copy research only and E3 governs real write authority;
- unknown data remains preserved and read-only; and
- external delivery work stays dormant until a new G0.

## 15. Decisions intentionally deferred to evidence

The following are not missing planning decisions:

- which non-Gold domains are valuable;
- which raw leaves are authoritative, mirrored, derived, or merely correlated;
- the exact safe write set for each selected operation;
- the simplest writer that passes measured hazard and performance limits;
- the final product IA derived from selected user tasks; and
- whether the product should ever move beyond personal and private use.

Atlas v0 and later controlled experiments must provide the evidence for those decisions.

## 16. Plan Persistence and Handoff Gate

No execution increment may move from `pending` to `in_progress` until its current plan is
committed and pushed to the shared branch. The persisted handoff must include:

- scope and explicit exclusions;
- measurable acceptance criteria and stop conditions;
- dependencies and current status;
- expected repository-safe and private outputs;
- authority and user-confirmation points;
- unresolved risks or disagreements; and
- the command or procedure another contributor uses to resume.

If review changes any of those items, persist the revised plan before execution continues.
Local-only plans, conversation history, and session task state are not sufficient handoff
artifacts.

## 17. Independent Increment Release Gate

An execution increment remains `in_progress` after its outputs and acceptance checks complete. It
may move to `done`, and the next increment may begin, only after an independent subagent reviews
the complete release candidate against its persisted plan and reports no findings.

The review covers outputs, acceptance evidence, safety and privacy boundaries, regression risk,
and handoff completeness. Every finding, including a non-blocking or documentation finding, must
be resolved or handled through an explicitly approved and persisted scope change. The resulting
completion state is then reviewed again. This loop continues until the independent reviewer
returns `No findings`.

The repository-safe release-gate record must contain:

- increment identifier and final outcome;
- full candidate commit and Git tree identifiers;
- governing plan path and persisted plan commit;
- reviewed path set and acceptance criteria;
- reviewer role or subagent identifier plus an independence attestation;
- every review iteration, finding disposition, and final `No findings` result;
- validation commands or procedures and their outcomes; and
- any private evidence represented only by repository-safe aliases and aggregate facts.

The reviewed candidate must be committed before the final review. The release-record commit must
use that candidate as its first parent and change only the release-gate record. Handoff verification
must reject the record if the candidate identifiers, parent relationship, or changed-path
restriction do not match. Any other change creates a new candidate and invalidates the prior
result.

Project-leader approval does not waive this gate.
