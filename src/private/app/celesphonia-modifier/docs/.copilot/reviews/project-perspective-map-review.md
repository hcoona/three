# Project perspective-map review

This record summarizes the review and adjudication that produced the final first-level
perspective taxonomy in `../plans/project-perspective-map.md`.

## Purpose and decision rule

First-level perspectives require independent durable decisions, distinct evidence standards,
and distinct failure consequences. Teams, crafts, roles, phases, and artifacts alone do not
justify a perspective.

## Process

1. Initial multi-agent brainstorm.
2. Ten specialist lenses to widen coverage and surface missing candidates.
3. Independent challenge of overlaps, omissions, and false first-level categories.
4. Second eight-agent brainstorm focused on completeness and parsimony.
5. Candidate synthesis into one proposed map.
6. Six independent reviews for completeness, parsimony, abstraction, domain fit, execution
   value, and adversarial balance.

## Final decision

The final adjudication kept exactly 20 first-level perspectives. The result was judged
complete and parsimonious.

## Label and boundary refinements

- #3: `Ecosystem legitimacy and social license` separates social credibility and disclosure
  expectations from product strategy and legal permission.
- #6: `Save-system representation and runtime truth` separates fingerprint-specific runtime
  claims from game-domain semantics.
- #8: `Product outcome definition and learning interpretation` separates outcome meaning and
  decision implications from strategy selection and research execution.
- #17: `Release identity and delivery-lifecycle contract` clarifies the authenticated
  release, install, update, repair, rollback, and removal contract.
- #18: `Supported-life resilience and operational-risk policy` clarifies readiness,
  response, maintenance, and sunset obligations without implying live-service operations.
- #20 clarification: authority, succession, release-stop and exception rights, and
  reconstructable rationale belong here; routine records administration does not.

These refinements sharpened boundaries without adding, removing, splitting, or merging
first-level perspectives.

## Rejected structural changes

- Merge #9 and #10: rejected.
- Merge #5 and #6: rejected.
- Merge #1 and #8: rejected.
- Fold #3 into #1: rejected.
- Remove #16 into cross-cutting assurance only: rejected.

## Integrated delivery routing

Integrated delivery is a program function, not perspective #21. Route value and scope to
#1, support promise to #7, evidence readiness to #16, release contract to #17,
affordability and capacity to #19, and integrator, change, stop, and residual-risk
authority to #20.

## Domain-fit invariants

- Normal editing is offline.
- Save-derived data is local by default.
- Proprietary game assets are excluded.
- Governance and operations are realized proportionally for a small utility rather than as
  enterprise bureaucracy.

## Explicit adjudications

The lone parsimony dissent and the adversarial delivery-authority concern were explicitly
adjudicated rather than ignored.

Candidate maps, raw brainstorm notes, and individual review drafts are intentionally not
copied here because they are superseded or redundant.
