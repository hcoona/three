## 4. Accepted authority model

The plan requires:

- approved A0 revision 3 to be the sole corpus authority;
- exact binding to its public project-leader approval commit;
- separate request digests for canonical manifest and inventory bytes;
- one strict baseline inventory row without inventing a per-row digest;
- strict JSON, lexical, role, decision, path, reparse, and internal-count validation;
- one selection-rule parser shared by manifest validation and live matching;
- first-match group order and exact live path, membership, and decision equality;
- verbatim preservation of all approved roots, aliases, rules, semantics, and order;
- manifest-derived copy-plan and receipt reconciliation;
- per-file held-source length and SHA-256 copy proof; and
- a compact nonproduction synthetic fixture with overlapping valid rules.

The plan requires complete deletion of public corpus counts, frozen save and definition tuples,
factories, accessors, record types, exact validators, downstream exact-count consumers,
production-derived fixtures, and active duplicate-authority claims. Renaming or relocating that
machinery does not satisfy the removal proof.

Target identity, protocol semantics, schema revisions, supported roles, lexical domains, path
policy, and trusted-local-filesystem constraints remain production responsibilities.
