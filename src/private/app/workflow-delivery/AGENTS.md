# Workflow Delivery v3 Instructions

Before acting on any Workflow Delivery v3 request, read
[`docs/agent-handoff.md`](docs/agent-handoff.md) and follow its change-routing
table alongside the root [`AGENTS.md`](../../../../AGENTS.md) governance rules.

Workflow Delivery v3 is the sole normative source for workflow delivery work;
do not fill v3 decision gaps from retired v1/v2 designs unless a v3 record
explicitly requires mechanism extraction and revalidation. Closed proving
campaigns remain stopped, and repository governance does not reopen them or
grant autonomous release, publication, authentication, or access changes.
