profiles and attempts. They do not prove universal registry immutability or
provide generic production Ruby migration support. The owner's accepted
platform-reliance decision avoided duplicate/race probes. Exact-byte readback
detects observed mismatches; it does not enforce server immutability.

reports and hashes identify retained observations without exposing credential
