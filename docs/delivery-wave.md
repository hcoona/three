# Current Delivery Wave

This record is the sole positive work-authorization authority under the
[repository record policy](governance/record-system.md#work-authorization-and-concurrency).
Only its accepted `main` version grants work. Issues, PRs, sessions and proposed
edits cannot add to or enlarge a grant. Merge changes authorization; deletion
ends a grant. Git and the proposing PR retain the reason and history.

## Authorized Advancements

### Advance Workflow Delivery v3

- **Work carriers:** The Workflow Delivery v3 project's current Issues and PRs.
  This project-wide grant is independent of a particular session, machine,
  branch or worktree. Each carrier identifies its concrete advancement and
  applicable accepted inputs; it does not enlarge the project's scope.
- **Authorized outcome:** Continue Workflow Delivery v3 through delivery of its
  accepted requirements and designs, including research, requirements and design
  refinement, implementation, validation, independent review, documentation and
  normal protected PR delivery.
- **Governing inputs:** The current
  [v3 handoff](../src/public/lib/three-workflow-delivery-v3/docs/agent-handoff.md),
  [requirements](../src/public/lib/three-workflow-delivery-v3/docs/requirements.md),
  HLD, applicable MLDs, slice LLDs and domain review/delivery gates. Accepted
  dependencies govern execution; proposed designs do not authorize their own
  implementation. Preserve the distinction between completed npm work and
  subsequent v3 advancements, including the NuGet slice.
- **Concurrency:** Follow the existing agent-execution and recovery rules for
  delegated workers.
  Coordinate conflicting changes to shared authorities and refresh affected
  validation/review when accepted prerequisites change. Keep sessions, progress,
  check results and handoffs in their actual work carriers, not this Wave.
- **Effects and domain gates:** Normal Git/Issue/PR operations, public-source
  research, isolated implementation and applicable checks are permitted. This
  repository-level continuation grant supplies no missing concrete native-probe,
  dispatch, publication, administrative or access authorization. Apply existing
  domain-specific prerequisites and separately confirmed effect bounds before
  those operations; do not reuse spent grants or another slice's risk decisions.
- **Completion boundary:** End or replace this entry through a reviewed Wave
  change when the accepted v3 delivery outcome is complete or the owner changes
  its scope. Unrelated projects and unconfirmed product, trust or release-scope
  expansions are outside this grant.
