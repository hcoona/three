### Align Workflow v3 Tests with Design and Engineering Principles

- **Work carrier:** [Issue #802](https://github.com/hcoona/three/issues/802).
- **Accepted inputs:** Current v3 requirements, HLD, MLDs and slice LLDs routed
  by the [handoff](../src/public/lib/three-workflow-delivery-v3/docs/agent-handoff.md),
  [engineering principles](engineering/engineering-principles.md), and the
  [delivered assessment and corrections](https://github.com/hcoona/three/issues/797#issuecomment-5758558863).
  Earlier classifications and retained dispositions are evidence to evaluate,
  not proof that the remaining tests have sufficient responsibility boundaries.
- **Authorized outcome:** Assess and correct the complete current supported v3
  suite against its accepted design and engineering principles. Cover Repository
  Model/Providers, CI, Release, records/admission, shared mechanisms,
  CLI/workflow contracts, dependency adapters, current native acceptance tooling
  and historical readers. Distinguish core rules, external input admission,
  trusted composition, fresh context/effect boundaries, dependency assumptions,
  public API compatibility and incidental implementation detail. Deliver
  justified test/fixture simplifications and necessary internal production
  corrections under existing contracts. No case-removal quota is selected.
- **Ordered prerequisites:** Bind current source, authorities, case/function
  identities and fixture/helper consumers. Before changing a family, record its
  actual invariants, consumers, sufficient owners and assertion dispositions;
  split mixed responsibilities in the assessment rather than treating module
  labels as test-level evidence. Independently adjudicate material findings and
  complete applicable design prerequisites before implementation. Preserve or
  transfer unique required assertions and validate affected owners/consumers
  before dependent groups. Permit independent delegated review and bounded
  execution under [agent execution guidance](engineering/agent-execution.md).
- **Preserved boundaries:** Keep public CLI/Python compatibility, current
  npm/NuGet support, schemas and canonical public bytes/digests, independent
  external/current-context admission, domain rule and deadline/concurrency
  semantics, authorization and irreversible-effect ordering, one-shot and
  ambiguous-result handling, supported historical readers and original
  evidence/provenance. Explicit representation requirements remain in force.
  The retired producer remains retired. New or changed contracts, support,
  compatibility, architectural authority or effects require separate owner
  disposition and accepted authorization. Do not hide cases in loops,
  skip/filter required validation, weaken required assertions or replace real
  dependency evidence with simulations.
- **Validation and effects:** Permit local source/test/document changes,
  existing validation/dependency operations, independent review and ordinary
  Issue/PR delivery. Validate complete affected groups and shared consumers,
  then the full v3 suite, applicable root HK, normal hooks and applicable
  hosted/automatic postmerge gates. Retain real Git/HK/SDK/process/loopback
  owners and [#721](https://github.com/hcoona/three/issues/721) task-resource
  lifecycle, including failed/interrupted evidence. No native service probe,
  dispatch/rerun, publication, credential/access/admin change,
  dependency-definition/schema/workflow/HK change, resource-limit change,
  host cleanup or unrelated process termination is authorized.
- **Completion:** Retain recoverable exact-source responsibility and change
  evidence, actual validation and independent domain/records/evidence reviews.
  Reassess the whole suite and reconcile every material finding, including
  questionable design constraints and owner decisions; unresolved necessity
  must not become a completed disposition. Green checks, a coarse distribution
  or an exhausted candidate list do not establish completion. End the finite
  grant by reviewed Wave deletion or replacement after verified delivery.
  Report actual case/resource observations without unmeasured saving claims.
